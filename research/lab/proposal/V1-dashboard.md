## Versi bahasa Inggris

> **Subject: Phased dashboard build — scope first, then fixed milestones**
>
> Hi,
>
> I build data dashboards where the hard part is the data, not the charts: ingest from an
> API or database, validate it, then serve it. A public example is a market research site
> I maintain, where data is collected every 15 minutes, cross-checked against the exchange,
> and published as static report pages:
> https://satgas.ai.id/crypto/riset/
>
> Before quoting your full budget I would run a short paid scoping step, because a
> $12,500-$37,500 range usually means the scope is much larger than the first version needs
> to be.
>
> **Step 0 — Scoping (2 days, $300).** One call, then a written spec: data sources, the
> three screens that matter first, the metrics each one shows, and acceptance criteria. You
> keep the document even if we stop there.
>
> **Step 1 — Working dashboard (2 weeks, $1,540).** Authentication, the three screens from
> Step 0, one data source wired end to end, deployed on your infrastructure. Not a mockup:
> real numbers from your data.
>
> **Step 2 — Hardening (1 week, $550).** Loading and error states, input validation,
> scheduled refresh, a health check that tells you when the data stopped arriving, and a
> short runbook.
>
> **Step 3 — Optional (as needed).** Extra screens or sources, quoted per screen after
> Step 1.
>
> Two questions before we start:
>
> - Where does the data live today: Postgres, MySQL, a spreadsheet, or an internal API?
> - Who uses the dashboard, and what decision does it help them make?
>
> Stack: Python (FastAPI), React, PostgreSQL or SQLite for small volumes. I work in
> English and can overlap with your timezone for calls.
>
