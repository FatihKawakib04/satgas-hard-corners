Conclusion

Look-ahead: clean. At 15 bps cost, Sharpe 0.97 and CAGR 24.5%. At 25 bps cost, Sharpe 0.93. 12-month holdout: strategy +7.6% vs BTC -32.4%. Null test significant on 1 of 2 block lengths. DSR 0.01 (needs >= 0.95 for a strong claim). Conclusion: the evidence is not yet sufficient to consider this a lasting edge.
