Audit E20: funding carry

A strategy that does not guess price direction: buy spot, short perpetual, harvest funding payments. The previous walk-forward reported Sharpe 2.30 and that is precisely the reason to be suspicious. This page recomputes from scratch with strict timing, two-leg costs, stress tests, and data checks against the exchange.

Conclusion

Funding carry is real, but small, and shrinking. Always active generates +11.72% over three years (+3.9% per year) after costs. Its Sharpe is high because volatility is almost zero, not because returns are large. What is not proven is the timing overlay used by E17: the threshold selected from training data produces -1.02% in the next two years (Sharpe -1.22). Status: not yet conclusive as a source of alpha, only suitable as a low-risk cash product with notional 0.5x.

1. Data audit

Before trusting the results, the data is checked first: spacing between settlements, duplicates, and match with the exchange.

  • Range: 2023-10-26 to 2026-10-25, 3286 8-hour intervals per asset, for BTC, ETH, and SOL.
  • Spacing between settlements is exactly 8 hours across all 3285 consecutive pairs, zero duplicates, settlement hours only 00, 08, and 16 UTC.
  • Matches Binance funding endpoint for six test points (March 2024, January 2025, June 2026): 6 of 6 identical to eight decimals.
  • Spot prices in the database compared with Binance klines: 2,201 dates, zero difference above 0.2%.

What is not available: historical perpetual prices. The deriv table only stores open interest and long/short ratio since September 2026, so the spot-perp basis cannot be measured and is modeled as an assumption in the stress test. This is a real limitation, not a detail.

2. Look-ahead: old version versus strict version

The old carry version determines today's position using an average funding that includes today's payment as well. That is logically wrong, so I measured its impact:

3. Costs and what is actually received

Each transaction has four cost components: spot fee 10 bps, spot slippage 5 bps, perp fee 5 bps, perp slippage 5 bps. Total 25 bps per direction on notional, so 50 bps round-trip.

Negative basis means the perpetual is trading cheaper than spot on entry and exit, so carry has to bear that difference. A basis of just 20 bps already cuts nearly half the profit.

9. Capital, liquidation, and real risks

With a notional of 0.5x capital, the perpetual short runs at 1x leverage: margin equal to notional, liquidation only occurs if the price rises by around 100% without additional margin. That is why its drawdown looks small. But the consequence is that the return is also small.

Historical rally: BTC once rose +60% in 30 days, ETH +70%, SOL +120%. At a notional of 0.75x, a rally like that would already pass the liquidation point if margin is not added from the spot leg. Delta-neutral does not mean risk-free; the risk moves to operations.

A more honest comparator for carry is not BTC, but deposits or short-term debt securities. If risk-free instruments yield 4 to 5% per year, a carry of 3.8% before tax and operational costs is not worth doing.

11. Limitations

  • Only three years and three assets. 2021-2022 is missing, so carry during an extreme bull and deep bear is not represented. Result status: not yet conclusive for periods other than 2023-2026.
  • The spot-perp basis is not measured, only assumed through scenarios. If the real basis is more negative than 20 bps, the figures above are too optimistic.
  • One exchange. Exchange risk, changes in funding rules, and margin maintenance are not modeled.
  • Taxes, transfer costs, and the opportunity cost of capital are not included.
  • Funding is a rate set by the exchange, and its mechanism can change at any time.

12. Decision

E17 is not pursued as an alpha candidate. The claimed Sharpe of 2.30 is rejected because it comes from a timing overlay that failed blind testing. Carry is still recorded as a low-risk cash instrument with realistic figures: 3.8% per year in 2023-2026, falling to around 0.7% in 2026. No new paper trading for carry until historical basis data exists.

The next step is not to add more carry experiments, but to decide economically: if the target is to cover VPS costs, a carry of 0.5x notional will not be enough. If the target is to hold cash with low risk, this is worse than a deposit. Both require a decision from Fatih, not additional experiments.
