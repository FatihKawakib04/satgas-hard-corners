Trading: limits and automatic stop

Current status: paper trading only. No live funds, no leverage, no loans, no API withdrawals.

Conditions before live capital may be used:

  1. strategy passes validation: DSR minimum 0.95 and both null tests significant
  2. paper trading for at least 60 days with drawdown within limits
  3. daily loss limit 2 percent of capital, total loss limit 10 percent
  4. automatic shutdown if limits are exceeded or price data fails

Loss limit: 2 percent of capital per day, 10 percent total. If the limit is exceeded or price data fails, the system stops and does not open new positions. There is no transaction target to cover VPS costs; if there is no valid signal, there is no transaction.

What cannot yet be estimated

  • Which sports and competitions are used. Cricket, football, and tennis have very different data availability.
  • The data source used and who bears the cost.
  • Output format: PDF, Google Sheets, or dashboard. All three have different work-hour requirements.
  • How deep the situational analysis requested is: toss and weather only, or including player injury tracking.

Therefore, the honest range is 18 to 30 hours, not a single number. The only things that can be confirmed so far are tasks that do not depend on the answers above: the data layer and the basic template.
