Conclusion

Look-ahead: clean. At a cost of 15 bps, Sharpe 0.69 and CAGR 14.8%. At a cost of 25 bps, Sharpe 0.65. 12-month holdout: strategy +0.2% vs BTC -32.4%. Null test significant on 0 of 2 block lengths. DSR 0.00 (needs >= 0.95 for a strong claim). Conclusion: the evidence is not yet sufficient to consider this a durable edge.
