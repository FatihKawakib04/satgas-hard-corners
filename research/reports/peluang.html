Economic opportunities

A list of revenue paths assessed from market evidence, not wishful thinking. The order follows the ratio between probability of success and time until money comes in. The fastest is selling skills, not waiting for a trading strategy to beat the market.

  1. The strongest evidence currently supports services and bounties, not trading. Market rates for data work are far more certain than the return of any strategy.
  2. Strategies that pass lab testing remain in simulation status. There is no real money yet, and there will be none without explicit approval.
  3. Every opportunity has a stop criterion: if there is no sign of progress within the time limit, it is dropped and replaced, not forced.
P1 One-time services opportunity: medium status: not-started

Bounties and data/crypto competitions (Superteam Earn, hackathon)

Estimated outcome$100 - $2,000 per bounty, depending on scale
Time to first revenue1 to 3 weeks for the first payment
Why it makes senseSolana bounty platforms recorded total payouts of around $11.5 million and provided development prizes above $319 thousand in one period. The work matches our capabilities: data dashboards, analysis scripts, and automated research reports.
RiskOpen competition, subjective judging, and payments can be slow. Some bounties require Solana skills we do not yet have.
First stepCreate an account, filter data/analytics-themed bounties with prizes of $200-1,000, submit the first 2 submissions using this research pipeline as proof of work.

Evidence:

  • Superteam Earn, bounty list and total payouts
  • Superteam Earn, summary of amounts paid out
P2 Recurring services opportunity: high status: not-started

Freelance data engineering and dashboards

Estimated outcome$25 - $60 per hour at the early stage, rising after a public portfolio exists
Time to first revenue2 to 6 weeks for the first client
Why it makes senseFreelance data engineer rates in the global market are around $60 per hour for an established profile, while Upwork's guide cites a range of $10-20 for entry level and $100+ for specialists. We already have real proof of work: data pipelines, backtests, and a static site running on a VPS.
RiskIt takes time to find clients and communicate. Income is unstable, and clients can demand an expanding scope of work.
First stepBuild a one-page portfolio with three case studies (crypto dashboard, this research pipeline, KodeMaverick project), then send 10 small proposals.

Evidence:

  • Upwork, 2026 hourly rate guide
  • Upwork, example data engineer profile with a $60/hour rate
P3 Niche services opportunity: medium status: not-started

Strategy audit and backtest services (packaged product)

Estimated outcome$150 - $600 per audit report
Time to first revenue3 to 8 weeks
Why it makes senseMany traders buy indicators and strategies without testing transaction costs or walk-forward. We already have an engine that can test their claims with costs, slippage, and randomized tests. This is a service that beginners find hard to imitate because it requires method, not opinion.
RiskThe market is small and requires trust. We must firmly reject profit claims that fail testing, which can make prospective clients walk away.
First stepCreate one public audit example (a popular strategy, such as MA cross) using this lab format, make it a free sample, then offer the paid version.

Evidence:

  • Example market for paid indicators on TradingView, including technology costs for creators
  • TradingView pricing page (trading tool market context)
P4 Content products opportunity: low status: not-started

Subscription research (newsletter/Substack) on crypto and macro

Estimated outcome$0 - $300 per month in the first 6 months
Time to first revenue3 to 12 months, highly dependent on audience
Why it makes senseCrypto research analyst salaries in the job market range from $80 thousand to $175 thousand per year, a sign that there is demand for analysis. But a subscription product needs an audience first, and that is not bought with quality alone.
RiskRevenue is close to zero for a long time. It risks diverting time from paid work and adding platform costs.
First stepPublish this lab report openly for 8 weeks first, measure the number of readers. Only consider monetization if there are at least 200 regular readers.

Evidence:

  • Indeed, salary distribution for crypto research analysts
  • Wellfound, remote crypto analyst job openings
P5 Project funding opportunity: low-medium status: not-started

Grant for open research tooling

Estimated outcome$1,000 - $10,000 per grant, processing takes 1 to 3 months
Time to first revenue1 to 3 months
Why it makes senseThis laboratory is an open research tool that others can use: backtesting with costs, randomized testing, and automated publication. Open source projects like this fit ecosystem grant programs, but the opportunity depends on the quality of documentation and evidence of usage.
RiskThe selection process is long and often gives no reason for rejection. Do not count grants as revenue until the funds arrive.
First stepTidy up the repo into a reusable package (documentation, license, examples), gather 4 weeks of commit history, then apply to one grant program.

Evidence:

  • Superteam Earn also distributes grants and freelance work

Ground rules

  • No costs are incurred without permission. All initial steps on this list are free.
  • Revenue estimates are a range based on market evidence, not a promise. Actual results are recorded separately when money comes in.
  • Actual revenue will be recorded on the day-30 and day-60 evaluation pages along with proof of transfer or invoice, not claims.
